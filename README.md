\#\# Key Findings:

* Total tickets handled: 8,469  
* Average customer satisfaction: 2.99  
* Ticket volumes were evenly distributed across priority levels.  
* Customer satisfaction was broadly consistent across support channels.  
* Ticket volumes were relatively evenly distributed across support channels, with Email receiving the highest volume of tickets.  
* The dashboard provides a high-level view of customer support performance and ticket distribution across priorities, channels and statuses.
